Automation

Enable KillURL

Master switch — turn the entire extension off or on. When off, no detection, skipping, or DRM bypass runs on any page.

Auto-Skip Countdowns

Automatically skip countdown timers when a URL shortener page is detected.

Auto-Redirect to Destination

Automatically navigate to the final destination URL, bypassing the shortener page entirely.

Show Notifications

Display a notification when a countdown is detected or skipped.

Skip Delay (ms)

Wait this many milliseconds before auto-skipping. Increase if some sites need more time to load.

Aggressive Mode

Re-activate timer bypass after Cloudflare challenges complete. Enable this if shortener pages use Cloudflare protection that blocks normal skipping.

Auto-Remove Ad-Block Walls

Automatically remove "disable your ad blocker" overlays and popups on every page.

AdBlock Stealth

Fool adblock detectors into thinking no ad blocker is installed — bait ads report as loaded and visible. Fixes Continue buttons that stay dead while your ad blocker is on.

Screen Share DRM Bypass

Automatically bypass DRM video protection on streaming platforms (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, etc.) so video content is visible when screen sharing. Requires page reload after enabling.
